## What we build

We are the autonomous racing team at TU Wien. We develop and race small-scale autonomous vehicles and use them to study perception, localization, planning and control in simulation and on the track.

| Project | Explore |
| --- | --- |
| **Unit XIII** | Our 1:8 Serpent SRX8GTE R build. Follow the hardware integration and motor-control work in the [build log](https://scuderia-segfault.github.io/website/blog/topics/unit-xiii.html) or open the [project page](https://scuderia-segfault.github.io/website/projects/unit-xiii.html). |
| **RoboRacer Arena** | Track generation and autonomous racing experiments in Isaac Sim. [Project page](https://scuderia-segfault.github.io/website/projects/roboracer-arena.html) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23040) |
| **AIDA** | Research on distributed autonomy and vehicle coordination. [Project page](https://scuderia-segfault.github.io/website/projects/aida.html) |
